# Green Racks — Acceptable Use Policy (Schedule B)

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to all services provided by
Green Racks Pty Ltd (ABN 23 696 631 071) ("Green Racks"), including Managed
WordPress Hosting, Colocation, Managed Colocation, Dedicated Servers, Managed
Servers, and AI Infrastructure. It forms part of the Service Agreement. "You"
means the Client and anyone the Client allows to use the services.

## 1. The basics

You may use the services for any lawful purpose consistent with your plan's
inclusions. You are responsible for all use of your services, including use by
your own customers and end users, and for keeping your credentials, keys, and
management interfaces (including iDRAC/iLO) secure.

## 2. Prohibited content

You must not store, publish, or transmit through the services:

- Content that is unlawful to possess or distribute in Australia, including
  child sexual abuse material (which will be reported to authorities without
  notice), or content that incites violence or terrorism.
- Content that infringes copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property
  rights, including unlicensed software, media, or warez distribution.
- Malware, ransomware, exploit kits, phishing pages, or command-and-control
  infrastructure.
- Content you are contractually or legally prohibited from holding (e.g. card
  data outside PCI-DSS compliance, health records without required safeguards).

## 3. Prohibited activities

You must not use the services to:

- Send unsolicited commercial electronic messages in breach of the
  **Spam Act 2003 (Cth)** — all marketing email must have consent, identify the
  sender, and include a functional unsubscribe facility. Bulk mail may only be
  sent to opt-in lists.
- Conduct denial-of-service attacks, port scanning, vulnerability scanning, or
  penetration testing of any system you do not own or have written
  authorisation to test.
- Attempt to access, disrupt, or interfere with other Green Racks customers'
  services, data, or network traffic.
- Forge headers, spoof source addresses, announce IP space or ASNs you are not
  authorised to use, or run open resolvers, open relays, or open proxies.
- Host services whose primary purpose is to conceal unlawful activity
  (bulletproof hosting, stolen-credential markets, carding forums).

## 4. Resource and network use

- Use must stay within your plan's allocations (traffic, power, storage).
  Sustained excess use may be billed as overage at Schedule A rates or require
  a plan upgrade.
- Activities that materially degrade shared infrastructure (including sustained
  100% resource saturation on shared platforms, or cryptocurrency mining on
  Managed WordPress or shared platforms) are not permitted. On dedicated or
  colocated hardware, high-intensity workloads are permitted within your
  contracted power and network allocations.
- Email from hosted services must comply with clause 3; Green Racks may apply
  outbound rate limits and block port 25 by default on new services.

## 5. Colocation-specific rules

- Only hardware disclosed to and accepted by Green Racks may be installed.
  Equipment must be safe, rack-compatible, and compliant with Australian
  electrical standards.
- Power draw must not exceed the contracted feed. Green Racks may de-energise
  equipment that poses a safety, fire, or stability risk to the facility, with
  notice where practicable.
- Site access is by arrangement per your service tier; you must follow facility
  security and induction procedures.

## 6. Security obligations

- Keep operating systems and exposed software patched on services you manage
  (unmanaged colocation, dedicated servers without a managed add-on).
- Notify Green Racks promptly at the abuse contact below if you become aware of
  a compromise of your services.
- Green Racks may quarantine or rate-limit a compromised service to protect the
  network, and will notify you as soon as practicable.

## 7. Enforcement

- Green Racks may investigate suspected AUP breaches and will ordinarily notify
  you and allow 48 hours to remedy.
- For breaches causing immediate harm (attacks in progress, unlawful content,
  network abuse, safety risk), Green Racks may suspend or de-energise the
  affected service immediately and notify you afterwards.
- Repeated or material breaches are grounds for termination under clause 8 of
  the Service Agreement. Fees remain payable during suspension caused by your
  breach.
- Green Racks will cooperate with lawful requests from Australian law
  enforcement and regulators (including ACMA and the eSafety Commissioner).

## 8. Reporting abuse

Report suspected abuse of Green Racks services to: **abuse@green-racks.com**
(confirmed live and monitored, 2026-07-05).

Green Racks may update this AUP with 30 days' notice; continued use of the
services after the notice period constitutes acceptance.
